Before writing, approving, delegating, or resuming any plan, brief, feature list, handoff, or todo list with 3+ steps, read `skill://plan-guard` and run its gates; output its verdict block. Also run it when anyone proposes new infrastructure, security, services, users, abstraction, or scale, and after two failed fixes on one premise. Skip only for one-sentence, one-or-two-file changes, and say so.
